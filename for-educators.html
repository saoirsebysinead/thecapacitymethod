The Gap Nobody Talks About

You've seen this.

Someone with a great idea. Real interest in the room. Every reason to act.
And they still don't.

They call it lack of confidence. But watch what actually happens: they freeze mid-sentence. They overexplain. They apologise before they've said anything. They leave the room without pitching — and only later realise someone would have bought.

That isn't a knowledge problem. It isn't a mindset problem. It's the nervous system reading the situation as a threat and overriding everything else. Visibility. Judgement. Uncertainty. Failure. The body responds before logic gets a chance.

This is the gap between knowing and doing. And most business programmes weren't built to close it.

Where This Comes From

Built from the inside out.

Capacity to Start is the first workshop in The Capacity Method — currently in its pilot phase with pre-start founders. If you’ve come from that page, or taken the quiz yourself, you’ve already seen what participants experience firsthand.

I built this after my own experience of burnout, grief, and rebuilding — and after retraining as a personal trainer and breathwork instructor. What I discovered was that the same physiological patterns I'd seen in my own breakdown were showing up in entrepreneurs. And that business education had no tools to address them.

The Capacity Method is designed to sit alongside what you're already doing well. Not to replace strategy or planning — but to close the gap between knowing and doing that strategy alone can't close.

The Method

Psychophysiological. Practical. Repeatable.

This isn't motivation training. It isn't stress management. It isn't mindset coaching.
It works at the level those approaches don't reach.

01

Nervous System Awareness

Recognising stress responses — mental and physical — as they activate, rather than after the damage is done. Awareness is the first step to choice.

02

Pattern Interruption

Identifying the sabotage patterns that show up under pressure — avoidance, overthinking, self-criticism — and learning to interrupt them in real time.

03

Aligned Action

Body and mind working with what the business actually requires — not forcing, not pushing through. Sustainable action from a regulated system.
