Why This Works

I didn't find this in a textbook.

I spent 15 years in HR systems before I burnt out — properly. My Nan died. My Mum died. My marriage collapsed. I was left with a mortgage to pay on my own. I didn't want to cope. I just wanted it to stop.

My coping mechanism? Wine. Lots of it. I'm not proud of it, but I'm not ashamed either. It's what I had at the time. No judgement from me — I've been there.

What eventually shifted things wasn't therapy or mindset work. It was my body. Strength training. Martial arts. Breathwork. I was genuinely surprised — I came for the physical, and something much deeper changed.

I retrained as a personal trainer and started working with clients. I saw serious shifts — not just fitness. Lives changing. I went deeper into fascia training, breathwork, and positive intelligence to understand what was actually happening at a system level.

Then I started attending business programmes. And I saw the exact same pattern I'd seen in my own breakdown — in entrepreneurs. People with good ideas, willing buyers in the room, and they still couldn't act. Everyone called it confidence. I knew it wasn't.

That's when I built The Capacity Method. Not as a coaching product. As a missing piece — designed to work alongside the business support you're already getting.

What Actually Happens

This isn't a talk. It's an experience.

Capacity to Start is a half-day workshop where you actually feel the difference between being stuck in protection mode and operating from capacity. No theory. No sitting still for three hours.

Here's what we do:

Shift 01

Recognise

Spot when your system flips into protection mode — physically and mentally. You can't change what you can't see. This is where awareness starts.

Shift 02

Identify

Name your sabotage patterns. Most people have two archetypes that show up under pressure. We'll find yours — not so you can fix yourself, but so you can work with them.

Shift 03

Choose

Learn to interrupt the pattern in real time. Not by forcing yourself, but by understanding the signal. This is where capacity replaces willpower.

We use breathwork and body-based exercises — not generic ones, but specific to the actions you're avoiding in your business. Starting a conversation. Making an offer. Showing your work. Whatever yours is.

There's usually a moment in the room where something shifts. You realise it's simpler than you thought. You can choose how you want to respond — rather than just react.

You Leave With

Something you can actually use.

  • Your specific sabotage patterns — named and recognised
  • A personal practice that works for you, not generic advice
  • The ability to spot protection mode activating — before it derails you
  • A way to interrupt the pattern in the moment — not hours later
  • The realisation that capacity is trainable, not fixed

This isn't motivation. It's not mindset. It's nervous system training — and it works when it matters, not just when you feel ready.
