Why This Works

I didn't find this in a textbook.

I spent 15 years in HR systems before I burnt out — properly. My Nan died. My Mum died. My marriage collapsed inbetween. All in the space of just over a year. I was left with a mortgage to pay on my own — while my career crumbled. I didn't want to cope. I just wanted it to stop. And I came very close to ending it all.

My coping mechanism? Wine. Lots of it. I'm not proud of it, but I'm not ashamed either. It's what I had at the time. No judgement from me — I've been there.

What eventually shifted things wasn't therapy or mindset work. It was my body. Strength training. Martial arts. Breathwork. I was genuinely surprised — I went for the physical — to feel stronger... but something much deeper changed. I no longer felt stuck in a hole I couldn't get out of. I started to move... forward with my life. But the pattern was clear. Everything that had truly helped me was body-based.

Inspired by my own experience, I retrained as a personal trainer and breathwork instructor. Started working with clients — using a fusion of modalities. I saw serious shifts. Not just fitness. Lives changing. Clients of 65 years climbing mountains — literally, not just figuratively. I went deeper into fascia training, breathwork, and nervous system regulation. I wanted to understand the whole system — not just the symptoms. And what I found changed how I saw everything.

Then I started attending business programmes. And I saw the exact same pattern I'd seen in my own breakdown — in aspiring entrepreneurs. People with good ideas, with willing buyers even in the room, and they still couldn't act. Everyone called it confidence or fear. I knew it wasn't.

That's when I built The Capacity Method. Not as a coaching product. As a missing piece — designed to work alongside business strategy/support or testing a business idea.

My name's Sinead, and if any of this resonates, you're in the right place.

What Actually Happens

This isn't a talk. It's an experience.

Capacity to Start is a half-day workshop where you actually feel the difference between being stuck in protection mode and operating from capacity. No theory. No sitting still for three hours.

Here's what we do:

Step 01

Recognise

See or feel when your system flips into protection mode — physically and mentally. You can't change what you can't see or feel. This is where awareness starts.

Step 02

Identify

Name your protection pattern — your own words, not someone else's framework. Not so you can fix yourself, but so you can work with what's actually there.

Step 03

Choose

Learn to interrupt the pattern in real time — and apply the right shift for the moment that matters. Posting your work. Making an offer. Having a hard conversation. This is where capacity replaces willpower.

The Tools

How Your Nervous System Shifts

Find your balance in a world that never slows down. These aren't generic relaxation techniques — they're practical tools specific to the actions required to start a business or test a business idea — before building.

Upshift

Activate

When you need energy, focus, and readiness. Use this before pitching, posting, or speaking — any moment that requires you to be seen or heard.

Downshift

Restore

When you need to come down from activation. Use this after high-pressure moments, decision fatigue, or when you're spiralling and need to reset.

Fullshift

Balance

When you need both — grounded clarity while staying present. Use this for starting the day regulated-ready, hard conversations, big decisions, or any moment where you need to stay calm and sharp at once.

You'll experience all three shifts in the workshop — using breathwork, awareness and body-based exercises that are specific to the business actions you're working with. Not generic wellness. Practical tools you can use the same day.

There's usually a moment in the room where something shifts. You realise it's simpler than you thought — even if it's not easy or comfortable. You can choose how you want to respond — rather than just react.
